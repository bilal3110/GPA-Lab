What is Semester GPA (SGPA)?

Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) measures a university student’s academic achievement in a single academic term (e.g., Fall 2026 or Spring 2027). Unlike Cumulative GPA, which spans your entire academic history, Semester GPA evaluates only the courses enrolled in that specific term.

Your semester GPA determines critical milestones every term, including:

  • Dean's Honour Roll / Semester Honours: Typically awarded for achieving an SGPA of 3.50 or higher in a full credit load.
  • Academic Good Standing: Maintaining at least a 2.00 SGPA to prevent academic warning or probation.
  • Scholarship Continuance: Many academic and sports scholarships require an SGPA above 3.00 or 3.20 each term.

How Semester GPA is Calculated: Quality Points & Credits

Each course you take carries two values: a credit hour weighting (usually 1, 2, 3, or 4 credits) and a grade point (from 0.0 to 4.0). When multiplied together, they yield Quality Points.

Semester GPA Formula
Semester GPA = ∑ (Course Grade Points_i × Course Credits_i) / ∑ (Course Credits_i)
  • Course Grade Points: Numerical value assigned to your letter grade (e.g., A = 4.0, B = 3.0).
  • Course Credits: Number of credit hours assigned to the lecture or lab.
  • Quality Points: Grade Points × Credit Hours. A 4-credit course with an A yields 16.0 quality points.
  • Total Credits: Sum of all credit hours attempted in the semester.

Worked Example: Real University Semester Calculation

Let's calculate the semester GPA for a student taking 5 courses in a standard university term:

Frequently Asked Questions About Semester GPA

How is Semester GPA (SGPA) calculated?

Semester GPA is calculated by multiplying each course's grade points by its credit hours to determine quality points, summing all quality points earned in that term, and dividing by the total semester credit hours.

What happens if I receive an F in a course?

An F grade contributes 0.0 grade points, but its credits remain in the denominator. For example, failing a 4-credit course produces 0 quality points while forcing your total quality points to be divided by 4 more credits, causing your GPA to plummet.

Do withdrawn (W) or Pass/Fail courses affect semester GPA?

Withdrawals (W) do not impact GPA calculations. Pass/Fail courses provide credit toward degree completion upon passing but carry no numerical grade points and are excluded from the GPA equation.

How many credits are typically taken in a university semester?

A standard full-time university semester typically consists of 15 to 18 credit hours (roughly 4 to 6 courses). Engineering and medical faculties often range up to 19 to 21 credit hours per semester.
